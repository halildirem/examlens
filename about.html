About ExamLens

ExamLens

✦ ✦ ✦

ExamLens was born from a simple but powerful observation: teachers spend an enormous portion of their professional lives marking papers — time that could be devoted to mentorship, lesson design, and human connection. We set out to change that.

We are a team of educators, linguists, and technologists united by a shared belief that artificial intelligence should serve the teacher, not replace them. ExamLens augments instructor expertise — it flags errors, codes them precisely, and presents findings in seconds — leaving the final pedagogical judgement exactly where it belongs: with the human expert in the room.

✦

Our Mission

To give every English instructor — whether in a metropolitan university or a rural preparatory school — access to evaluation tools of the same quality and speed previously available only to the most resource-rich institutions. We believe great feedback should not be a luxury.

What We Do

ExamLens uses Claude, Anthropic's frontier AI model, to analyse handwritten or typed English exam papers. In seconds, it:

  • Transcribes handwritten text with high fidelity
  • Identifies and codes every linguistic error (spelling, grammar, punctuation, word order, and more)
  • Highlights errors directly in the transcription with inline annotations
  • Generates a one-sentence professional assessment summary
  • Estimates a score and deduplicates repeated mistakes so reports stay clean

Our Values

  • Educator First: Every feature decision starts with "does this help the teacher?"
  • Honesty: We are transparent about what AI can and cannot do.
  • Privacy: Student work is processed and discarded — we do not train on your data.
  • Accessibility: A generous free tier and fair pricing mean any instructor can start today.
  • Continuous Improvement: Our error taxonomy grows with educator feedback.

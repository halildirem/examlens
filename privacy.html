Privacy Policy

ExamLens · Educator Evaluation Portal

✦ ✦ ✦

Plain-language summary: We collect only what is necessary to run the service. We do not sell your data. Student exam content is processed and immediately discarded — it is never stored on our servers beyond the duration of a single API call.

1. Who We Are

ExamLens ("we", "us", "our") is an AI-powered educator evaluation platform operated by ExamLens Ltd. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our web application at examlens.com and any related services.

2. Information We Collect

Account data: When you register, we collect your name, email address, and password (hashed). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google's OAuth service.

Profile data (optional): You may voluntarily provide your school name, birth date, and teaching level. These are stored in your account and are never shared with third parties.

Usage data: We store a summary of each evaluation (the AI's one-sentence assessment, error count, and timestamp) in your account for your personal history.

Payment data: Payments are processed exclusively by iyzico. We never see or store full card numbers. We record the plan purchased, amount, and transaction status.

Exam image content: Photographs or documents you upload are transmitted directly to Anthropic's API for processing and are not stored on ExamLens servers at any point.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the ExamLens evaluation service
  • To manage your account, credits, and evaluation history
  • To process payments and prevent fraud
  • To send service-related emails (receipts, account notices) — no marketing without consent
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Third-Party Services

  • Anthropic (Claude API) — processes exam images/text for grading.
  • Firebase (Google) — authentication and session management.
  • MongoDB Atlas — encrypted cloud database for account data.
  • iyzico — PCI-DSS compliant payment processing.
  • Render — cloud hosting provider for our backend servers.

5. Data Retention

Account data is retained while your account is active. You may request deletion at any time by emailing privacy@examlens.com. Evaluation history is capped at 20 entries and automatically overwritten as new evaluations are added. Payment records are retained for 7 years as required by financial regulations.

6. Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to processing or request restriction
  • Data portability

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@examlens.com.

7. Cookies

ExamLens uses only essential session cookies required for authentication. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels.

8. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS. Database access is restricted by network rules and authenticated credentials. Passwords are never stored in plain text.

9. Children

ExamLens is intended for use by adult educators. We do not knowingly collect data from persons under 16 years of age.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes by email or by displaying a prominent notice in the application.

11. Contact

For privacy enquiries: privacy@examlens.com
